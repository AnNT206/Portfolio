ABOUT

Curious about what happens
behind the screen.

I'm Ngo Thien An, and I've always had a passion for computers — not only for what they show on screen, but for the structure behind a piece of software: how data moves, how services talk to each other, and how a system stays correct when many things happen at once.

That curiosity led me to study Software Engineering at FPT University in Ho Chi Minh City, and pulled me toward backend development. Today I spend most of my time with Java and Spring Boot: designing REST APIs, modelling data with JPA/Hibernate, and learning to reason about transactions, concurrency and tests.

I'm in semester 5 of 9 and looking for a Java backend internship where I can learn from experienced engineers, contribute to real products, and keep getting better at building systems people can rely on.
